# 탐사 배경 음악 4곡 프롬프트 (로파이)

공통: 가사 없는 연주곡 · 끊김 없이 반복되는 루프 · 3분 안팎 · 걸으면서 들어도 지루하지 않게 잔잔하지만 전진하는 느낌 · 전투가 나올 때도 이어서 들려요(전투 효과음이 위에 얹혀요).
넣는 파일 이름: `public/audio/bgm_x1_forest.mp3` · `bgm_x2_crypt.mp3` · `bgm_x3_cave.mp3` · `bgm_x4_temple.mp3` (파일을 넣으면 그 지역 탐사에서 자동으로 나와요. 없으면 기본 곡이 계속 나와요)

## 1. 달빛 숲길 (초보 · 남쪽)
> Cozy lo-fi instrumental for a quiet night walk through a moonlit forest. About 78 BPM, laid-back swung drums with soft brushed snare, warm muted acoustic guitar plucks, gentle felt piano, a round sub bass, and a faint music-box shimmer like fireflies. Light vinyl crackle and soft crickets in the background. Hopeful, curious and safe, a little adventurous. Seamless loop, no vocals.

## 2. 무너진 지하 묘지 (중급 · 서쪽)
> Eerie but cozy lo-fi instrumental for exploring a ruined underground crypt. About 70 BPM, slow dusty boom-bap beat with a deep kick and a soft rim click, detuned Rhodes electric piano, hollow pipe-organ pads, a slow cello line, and distant cold bell tones with long reverb. Tape wobble, faint wind and dripping echo. Mysterious and slightly spooky, never scary. Seamless loop, no vocals.

## 3. 안개 호수 동굴 (고급 · 북쪽)
> Dreamy aquatic lo-fi instrumental for a foggy lake cave with glowing crystals. About 74 BPM, soft shuffling beat with muffled hi-hats, glassy FM bells, a watery chorused electric guitar, warm analog pad, and sparse harp arpeggios, with water droplets and a deep cave reverb. Cool blue and teal mood, calm and slightly lonely. Seamless loop, no vocals.

## 4. 호박달 고대 신전 (초고급 · 동쪽)
> Majestic yet mellow lo-fi instrumental for an ancient golden temple at the end of the road. About 82 BPM, steady head-nodding boom-bap beat with soft hand-drum and low frame-drum accents, plucked oud or sitar-like strings, warm brass pad, a deep gong now and then, and slow rising choir-like synth swells. Dusty, golden, solemn and a bit heroic, with tape saturation and light vinyl noise. Seamless loop, no vocals.
