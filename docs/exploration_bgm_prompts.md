# 탐사 배경 음악 4곡 프롬프트 (긴박한 칩튠 전투 느낌)

공통: 가사 없는 연주곡 · 끊김 없는 반복(루프) · 2~3분 · 걷는 중에도 전투 중에도 이어서 나와요.
파일 이름: `public/audio/bgm_x1_forest.mp3` · `bgm_x2_crypt.mp3` · `bgm_x3_cave.mp3` · `bgm_x4_temple.mp3` (넣으면 그 지역 탐사에서 자동으로 나와요)

## 1. 달빛 숲길 (초보)
Energetic 8-bit chiptune battle theme for the first dungeon, a moonlit forest. About 150 BPM, driving square-wave arpeggios, a punchy pulse-wave lead melody, bouncy triangle bass, crisp noise-channel snare and hi-hats. Bright and heroic with a hint of danger, like an early-game JRPG adventure. Tight seamless loop, no vocals.

## 2. 무너진 지하 묘지 (중급)
Tense dark chiptune battle theme for a haunted underground crypt. About 160 BPM, minor key, fast pulse-wave arpeggios, chromatic bass runs on triangle wave, eerie vibrato lead, heavy noise-channel drums with sharp snare rolls. Spooky and urgent, retro NES style. Tight seamless loop, no vocals.

## 3. 안개 호수 동굴 (고급)
Fast, icy chiptune battle theme for a foggy crystal cave with a lake. About 165 BPM, shimmering fast arpeggios, echoing pulse-wave lead with delay, pulsing sawtooth-like bass, rolling noise-channel drums, a cold and dangerous mood with watery bell-like blips. 16-bit console feel. Tight seamless loop, no vocals.

## 4. 호박달 고대 신전 (초고급)
Epic, intense chiptune boss-rush battle theme for an ancient golden temple. About 175 BPM, heroic wide pulse-wave lead, fast layered arpeggios, driving octave bass, double-time noise drums with cymbal-like crashes, dramatic key change in the second half. Final-dungeon energy, 16-bit style. Tight seamless loop, no vocals.
